## Built from real projects

The first knowledge base is being extracted from **Political World**, **TerraForge**,
**Scenario Tools**, **Grand Strategy**, and focused Modding Lab experiments.
Unfinished and failed projects are kept as research material when they reveal useful engine behavior.
