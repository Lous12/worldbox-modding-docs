WBML 1.0 is **not another runtime suite**. It is the consolidation/publication milestone after the 0.9 closure PASS.

**Frozen stack:** WorldBox `0.51.2` build `719` · Unity `2022.3.60f1` · NeoModLoader `1.2.0.1` · Political World `1.7.0` / PoliticalWorldAPI `1.14.0` where applicable.

## What “closed” means

The practical current-runtime surface is classified with explicit evidence boundaries: `VERIFIED`, `OBSERVED`, `UNSAFE`, or explicit `UNKNOWN`. It does **not** mean every one of the 18,625 methods was invoked or that all methods are safe.

Systematic research reopens only when:

1. WorldBox version/build changes;
2. NeoModLoader version changes; or
3. a direct contradiction to the frozen baseline is discovered.

A normal mod bug is not automatically a reason to restart WBML. Use the existing baseline first.

Machine consolidation: `/worldbox-modding-docs/data/wbml/1000-practical-modding-baseline.json`.
